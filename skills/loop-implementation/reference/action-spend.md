# Action-spend durability

Read this contract before admitting a return or restoring any saved ledger, including seed, row, companion and verification repairs. Keep action accounting separate from row attempts and repair allowances.

1. Before deleting a jobs file, resetting a checkout or restoring ledger decisions, retain every `ran <action> (<k> of <bound>)` and `bound spent: <action> (<k> of <bound>)` record from the child's jobs file, returned evidence/decisions and attributable ledger or commit body. Read only those permitted boundary artifacts, never a transcript or log. Treat action-record lines in the jobs file as accounting, not process entries; apply process ownership checks only to pid records. Require a pre-launch spend reservation, confirmed execution record or affirmative retained evidence that no paid/external action launched. With uncertain execution and no attributable reservation, stop at recovery incomplete with `action execution uncertain`; preserve state rather than infer an unspent bound from missing decision text.
2. Before admitting a successful row or seed return, require every reserved spend to survive in its ledger evidence/decisions (or companion result cells). For verification repairs, require the records after tuple/iteration in the attributed child commit body instead, leaving row cells unchanged; copy them into the home repair marker. A reservation without a confirmed `ran` record stays conservatively spent. A missing spend record is a validation miss, not permission to erase it. Retain the action records until admission or a durable repair marker; only then delete the jobs file after process cleanup.
3. For every interrupted or validation-miss repair, copy all retained action records into the parent repair commit body before clearing the pending tuple or discarding child output. Include them in a numbered target's evidence cell after the interruption/miss reason, within the existing result-line bound; the commit body retains the complete accounting. For seed preserve the saved row cells and fixed seed decision contract; keep action accounting in the `loop: repair seed` commit body. For verification retain it in the `loop: repair verification` marker. Keep prior decisions intact and add no decision kind or ledger header.
4. Carry these repair commit bodies as spend history on every later dispatch and authorized restore, including companion dispatches: inject home repair action records in the existing hint when the child cannot read home history. Count the highest recorded k for each action across decisions, evidence and repair bodies; identical copies are one spend. Preserve this history through retry, amendment and finish.

For a capture that succeeds before an interrupted seed, preserve `ran capture (1 of 1)` in the seed repair commit body even when reset removes its fixture and ledger changes. The next child counts that spend and records `bound spent: capture 1 of 1`, never another capture. Preserve a lone pre-launch reservation conservatively in the same way.
