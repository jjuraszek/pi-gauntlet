## Change
The council treats a deferred or deviates disposition without a quoted cut question and human answer as a scope blocker, even when its destination is a spec path. It blocks elsewhere attribution for an own-repo snapshot row and a production venue. A human-answered cut and another repo's settled attribution remain valid.
